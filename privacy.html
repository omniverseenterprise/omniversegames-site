Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information RogueBlocks: Monster Tactics ("the game"), published by Omniverse Enterprise ("we", "us"), handles and why.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not send your game data to us or to any server we run.

What stays on your device

Your progress (gold, monsters, unlocked stages, settings) and a log of your games (stages played, results, gold earned and spent, screen size) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or double your gold) and occasional full-screen ads between games. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google handles this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the game's Shop. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The game asks Google to show only ads rated PG (parental guidance) or lower.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game keeps a record of purchase receipts on your device so a purchase is never granted twice. Google Play's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Ad personalisation: use Ad privacy choices in the Shop (where offered) or your device's ad settings.
  • Any other request: email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information in a new way (for example, adding analytics), we will update this page and its effective date before that change ships.
